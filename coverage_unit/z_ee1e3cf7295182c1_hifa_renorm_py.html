6def hifa_renorm(vis=None, createcaltable=None, threshold=None, spw=None, excludechan=None, 

7 atm_auto_exclude=None, bwthreshspw=None, parallel=None): 

8 """Assess and optionally correct ALMA amplitude normalization errors caused by bright line emission. 

9 

10 When bright astronomical lines are present in the autocorrelations of science targets, the ALMA 

11 correlator's amplitude normalization can be incorrect, leading to under-scaled amplitudes in the 

12 affected channels. This task creates per-channel renormalization spectra (scaling spectra) and, 

13 optionally, stores them in a Tsys-like calibration table to be applied during :func:`~pipeline.hif.cli.hif_applycal`. 

14 (Prior to PL2024 the correction was applied by editing the MS directly.) 

15 

16 The algorithm compares the autocorrelations of the bandpass calibrator (assumed line-free) to those 

17 of the science target. After dividing out the bandpass autocorrelation, an iterative polynomial 

18 baseline fit (up to fifth order) is performed to isolate any line contamination and determine the 

19 per-channel rescaling needed. Spectral windows with many channels are split into fitting segments, 

20 and the code checks for features at segment boundaries and adjusts them accordingly. When 

21 ``atm_auto_exclude=True`` (default) any strong atmospheric features are excluded from the fit. 

22 

23 Renormalization is applied only when a scaling value > ``threshold`` (default: 1.02, i.e. > 2%) is 

24 found for a given target, spw, scan, field, antenna, and correlation. 

25 

26 Renormalization is not needed for TDM spectral windows. Bands 9 and 10 (double-sideband receivers) 

27 rarely require renormalization and are treated specially. 

28 

29 The WebLog main page table shows the maximum renormalization value found per target, per spw. Rows 

30 with values above the threshold are highlighted in blue (when ``createcaltable=True``) or red (when 

31 ``createcaltable=False``). Summary plots show the cumulative-average scaling spectra per field and 

32 spw. PDF files contain per-antenna, per-correlation diagnostic scaling spectra. A warning message 

33 'Number of Segments Changed!!!' appears on summary plots when the number of fitting segments varies 

34 across fields being averaged together. 

35

38 :alt: WebLog for hifa_renorm 

39 

40 WebLog for the ``hifa_renorm`` stage. Table rows highlighted blue indicate values 

41 above the threshold (default: 1.02 / 2%). Summary plots show cumulative-average 

42 renormalization spectra per field/spw. 

43

49 causes atypically high scaling > 1.25 (25%). 

50

53 :alt: Renormalization PDF with atmospheric exclusion 

54 

55 PDF where atmospheric regions are automatically excluded (semi-opaque grey). 

56 Excluded spectra appear as grey dashed lines; yellow boxes mark excluded 

57 frequency ranges. When no real astronomical lines are present, 

58 renormalization is not applied. 

59 

60 Notes: 

61 The QA score reflects the magnitude of the maximum scaling factor R_f found, relative to 

62 the threshold R_th (default: 1.02): 

63 

64 - R_f < R_th: QA = 1.0 - (1.0 - 0.90) x (R_f - 1.0) / (R_th - 1.0) -- green, 0.90-1.0 

65 - R_th <= R_f < 2.5: QA = 0.9 - (0.9 - 0.66) x (R_f - R_th) / (2.5 - R_th) -- blue, 0.67-0.9 

66 - R_f >= 2.5: QA = 0.66 -- yellow 

67 - Bands 9 and 10 receive a fixed QA = 0.9 (blue) regardless of R_f. 

68 - If R_f is erroneous or unrealistic (< 1 or > 2.5), an additional QA message is issued. 

69 

70 Examples: 

71 1. Run with recommended settings to assess the need for an ALMA amplitude renormalization 

72 correction. 

73 

74 >>> hifa_renorm() 

75 

76 2. Run to assess the necessary ALMA amplitude renormalization correction, and apply this 

77 correction if it exceeds a threshold of 3% (1.03). 

78 

79 >>> hifa_renorm(createcaltable=True, threshold=1.03) 

80 

81 """
