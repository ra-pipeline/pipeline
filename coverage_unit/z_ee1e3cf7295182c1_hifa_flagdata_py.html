10 """Do metadata based flagging of a list of MeasurementSets. 

11 

12 The ``hifa_flagdata`` task performs basic flagging operations on a list of MeasurementSets including: 

13 

14 - applying online flags (XML format, including QA0 flags for antenna pointing calibration failures) 

15 - applying a flagging template 

16 - partial polarization flagging 

17 - autocorrelation data flagging 

18 - shadowed antenna data flagging 

19 - scan-based flagging by intent or scan number 

20 - edge channel flagging, as needed 

21 - low atmospheric transmission flagging 

22 

23 The WebLog page shows whether any data in these categories were flagged (a check mark means yes, an X means no). 

24 The Flagged data summary table shows the percentage of flagged data per MS. The ``Before Task`` column contains 

25 only the effect of the Binary Data Flags (BDF) from the correlator applied during :func:`~pipeline.hifa.cli.hifa_importdata`. The 

26 additional flags are applied in the order of columns shown in the table, with each column reflecting the 

27 additional amount of data flagged when applying that flag reason. 

28 

29 The Low Transmission flagging agent flags spws whose transmission across 60% of their bandwidth is less than 

30 10% on non-representative spws and less than 5% on the representative spw. This heuristic can be disabled with 

31 the boolean value ``lowtrans`` in the PPR. The thresholds can be adjusted via ``mintransnonrepspws`` (default 

32 10%) and ``mintransrepspw`` (default 5%). The following fixed meteorological values are used to avoid 

33 susceptibility to faulty weather station values: pressure=563 mb, altitude=5059 m, temperature=273 K, 

34 maxAltitude=48 km, humidity=20%, water vapor scale height h0=1.0 km, initial pressure step dP=5.0 mb, and 

35 multiplicative factor dPm=1.1. The PWV is the actual median across the EB; the airmass is the mean of the 

36 first and final integration of the scan. 

37 

38 About the spectral window edge channel flagging: 

39 

40 - For TDM spectral windows, a number of edge channels are always flagged, according to the ``fracspw`` and 

41 ``fracspwfps`` parameters (the latter operates only on spectral windows with 62, 124, or 248 channels). With 

42 the default setting of ``fracspw``, the number of channels flagged on each edge is 2, 4, or 8 for 64, 128, 

43 or 256-channel spectral windows, respectively. 

44 

45 - For most FDM spectral windows, no edge flagging is done. The only exceptions are ACA spectral windows that 

46 encroach too close to the baseband edge. Channels that lie closer to the baseband edge than the following 

47 values are flagged: 62.5, 40, 20, 10, and 5 MHz for spectral windows with bandwidths of 1000, 500, 250, 125, 

48 and 62.5 MHz, respectively. A warning is generated in the weblog if flagging occurs due to proximity to the 

49 baseband edge. By definition, 2000 MHz spectral windows always encroach the baseband edge on both sides, and 

50 thus are always flagged on both sides in order to achieve 1875 MHz bandwidth (flagged by 62.5 MHz on each 

51 side); thus no warning is generated. 

52 

53 Notes: 

54 **QA Scoring** 

55 

56 The QA score based on BDF+QA0+online+template+shadow flags: 0.0 if the flag fraction is >=60%, 1.0 if 

57 <=5%, linearly interpolated in between. An additional QA score of 0.8 results if the baseband frequency 

58 range could not be calculated for a spw. For Low Transmission flagging: 1.0 if no spw is flagged, 0.9 if 

59 a non-representative spw is flagged, 0.33 if the representative spw is flagged. 

60 

61 Examples: 

62 1. Do basic flagging on a MeasurementSet: 

63 

64 >>> hifa_flagdata() 

65 

66 2. Do basic flagging on a MeasurementSet flagging additional scans selected 

67 by number as well: 

68 

69 >>> hifa_flagdata(scannumber='13,18') 

70 

71 """
