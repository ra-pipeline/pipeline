15 """Perform iterative self-calibration on science targets with sufficient signal. 

16 

17 Attempts phase-only self-calibration on each science target for which the estimated SNR per-EB 

18 per-antenna exceeds 3. The task channels-averages the science data to 15.625 MHz, flags 

19 line channels (from :func:`~pipeline.hif.cli.hif_findcont`), splits each source into a temporary MS, and then 

20 iterates through a series of gain solution intervals. 

21 

22 The pipeline tracks multiple data type labels to manage regular and self-calibrated data: 

23 

24 - ``REGCAL_CONTLINE_SCIENCE``: data with regular calibrations applied (``DATA`` column of 

25 ``*_targets.ms``). 

26 - ``SELFCAL_CONTLINE_SCIENCE``: data with self-calibration solutions applied (``CORRECTED`` 

27 column of ``*_targets.ms``). 

28 - ``REGCAL_LINE_SCIENCE`` / ``SELFCAL_LINE_SCIENCE``: equivalent line (continuum-subtracted) 

29 datatypes in ``*_targets_line.ms``. 

30 

31 **Solution interval sequence**: The first interval is always ``inf_EB`` (``combine='scan'``, 

32 ``solint='inf'``, ``gaintype='G'``), covering one entire EB and initially solving per-spw, 

33 per-polarization. Subsequent intervals use ``combine='spw'``, ``gaintype='T'``: ``inf`` 

34 (one solution per scan), intermediate intervals splitting the median scan time, and finally 

35 ``int`` (one solution per integration). The target is 5 total intervals including ``inf`` and 

36 ``int``. Only the final successful interval and ``inf_EB`` (if not the final) are applied; 

37 intermediate successful intervals are discarded. 

38 

39 **Solution acceptance**: A solution interval is accepted if all of the following hold: 

40 

41 - The synthesized beam area does not increase by more than 5% compared with the pre-selfcal 

42 image. 

43 - The SNR of the post-selfcal image exceeds the SNR of the pre-selfcal image. 

44 - The near-field SNR (rms measured in an annulus just outside the clean mask) also improves. 

45 - The rms does not increase by more than 5%. 

46 

47 If self-calibration succeeds, results are applied to both ``*_targets.ms`` and 

48 ``*_targets_line.ms``. The final image is cleaned to the minimum of ``3 x rms`` (from the 

49 final successful interval) and the pre-selfcal clean threshold. 

50 

51 The WebLog shows a summary table of solution intervals attempted, SNR/rms before and after 

52 each interval, and whether self-calibration succeeded. Per-interval QA plot pages show 

53 before/after images and gain solutions per EB and antenna. 

54 

55 .. figure:: /figures/selfcal_weblog.png 

56 :width: 60% 

57 :alt: Self-calibration WebLog 

58 

59 Example WebLog. The 'List of Self-cal Targets' table shows targets, imaging 

60 parameters, solution intervals, and success status. The 'Self-cal Target Details' 

61 section shows before/after SNR, rms, beam size, and images, and describes why 

62 self-calibration stopped. 

63 

64 Notes: 

65 QA scores: 

66 

67 - QA = 1.0 if self-calibration was not attempted (SNR too low). 

68 - QA = 0.99 if attempted but unsuccessful (solutions not applied). 

69 - QA = 0.98 if attempted and applied successfully. 

70 - QA = 0.85 if applied but the RMS got worse for at least one source. 

71 - QA = 0.90 if a new/experimental mode (e.g. mosaic self-calibration) was used. 

72 - QA = N/A for unsupported modes (e.g. ephemeris targets). 

73 

74 Examples: 

75 1. Run self-calibration on all science targets: 

76 

77 >>> hif_selfcal() 

78 

79 2. Run self-calibration on a single science target: 

80 

81 >>> hif_selfcal(field='3C279') 

82 

83 3. Use a more relaxed beam-size acceptance threshold: 

84 

85 >>> hif_selfcal(delta_beam_thresh=0.15) 

86 

87 """
