9 """Identify line-free continuum frequency ranges for each science target and spw. 

10 

11 Creates dirty image cubes at native channel resolution (using ``robust=1`` for optimal line 

12 sensitivity) for each science target and spw. The pipeline then runs the ``findContinuum`` 

13 function to identify channel ranges likely free of line emission via the following steps: 

14 

15 1. **Joint-mask mean spectrum**: SNR-based thresholds on the moment-0 (integrated) and 

16 moment-8 (peak) images define a 2-D joint mask. The mask is pruned to remove islands 

17 smaller than ``max(4, int(beamAreaInPixels * minbeamfrac))`` pixels (``minbeamfrac=0.3``; 

18 0.5 for ACA 7m if all islands are pruned). The mean spectrum over the mask is computed 

19 and analyzed to find line-free channels. 

20 2. **Pre-smoothing**: A boxcar smoothing kernel is applied to the mean spectrum prior to 

21 analysis. For Cycle 10+ data the kernel width is derived from the 

22 ``spectralDynamicRangeBandwidth`` ASDM attribute. For older data it is based on the 

23 ``nbin`` factor from the preceding :func:`~pipeline.hif.cli.hif_makeimlist` stage, with additional heuristics 

24 to skip smoothing for wide + narrow spw combinations, already-labeled continuum spws, 

25 and cases of strong line emission (peak SNR > 10: nbin limited to 2 for 12m, 3 for 7m). 

26 3. **Moment-difference contamination check**: Line-free channels are used to form ``mom8fc`` 

27 and ``mom0fc`` images; the scaled-subtracted ``momDiff`` image peak SNR is computed. 

28 If ``momDiffSNR > 8`` (or > 11.5 for high-atm-variation spws), contamination is likely. 

29 Two remediation paths are tried: **Amend Mask** (logic code starts with ``A``) or 

30 **Only Extra Mask** (code starts with ``E``). Further steps include channel intersection, 

31 extra-mask, and ``autoLower`` (``X``, ``Y``) iterations. The final logic path code and 

32 momDiffSNR are shown in the plot legend and the WebLog table. 

33 4. **AllContinuum check**: If a single range covers >= 92.5% of the channels (>= 91% for 

34 spws with < 75 channels), the spw is declared ``AllContinuum`` and no cube is subsequently 

35 cleaned. 

36 

37 If a ``cont.dat`` file already exists in the working directory, spws with pre-defined ranges 

38 are not re-analyzed; only spws not listed are processed. The resulting ``cont.dat`` file (LSRK 

39 frequency ranges) is used by subsequent :func:`~pipeline.hif.cli.hif_uvcontsub` and :func:`~pipeline.hif.cli.hif_makeimages` stages. 

40 

41 Using ``hm_mode='coarse'`` applies the fast-imaging override with reduced 

42 pixels-per-beam sampling to optimize processing speed. The default mode is 

43 ``hm_mode='normal'`` to preserve the previous-cycle behavior. 

44 

45 .. figure:: /figures/guide-img029.png 

46 :width: 60% 

47 :alt: Example findContinuum plots 

48 

49 Two examples: entire spectral window identified as continuum (left) and two 

50 continuum ranges identified (right). Cyan horizontal lines mark the identified 

51 frequency ranges. 

52 

53 Notes: 

54 QA = 1.0 if continuum frequency ranges were found for all spws; otherwise QA = fraction 

55 of spws for which a range was identified. QA = 0.0 if size mitigation previously failed. 

56 

57 Examples: 

58 1. Identify continuum frequency ranges for all science targets and spws: 

59 

60 >>> hif_findcont() 

61 

62 2. Perform continuum frequency range detection for a specific field: 

63 

64 >>> hif_findcont(field='M51') 

65 

66 """
