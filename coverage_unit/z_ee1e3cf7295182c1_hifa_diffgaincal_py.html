6def hifa_diffgaincal(vis=None, flagging_frac_limit=None, hm_spwmapmode=None, missing_scans_frac_limit=None): 

7 """Derive SpW phase offsets from differential gain calibrator. 

8 

9 This task creates the spectral window phase offset table used to allow 

10 calibrating the "on-source" spectral setup with phase gains from a 

11 "reference" spectral setup. Currently this setup with two different 

12 SpectralSpecs is used by the band-to-band mode, for a high and low 

13 frequency band. 

14 

15 A bright point source quasar, called the Differential Gain Calibrator 

16 (DIFFGAIN) source, is used for this purpose. This DIFFGAIN source is typically 

17 observed in groups of interleaved "reference" and "on-source" scans. These 

18 blocks typically occur once at the start and once at the end of the 

19 observation. In very long observations, there may be a group of scans 

20 occurring during the middle. 

21 

22 The procedure for the phase calibration process is as follow: 

23 

24 - ``reference`` calibration uses the "reference" spectral setup, the "low" 

25 frequency for band-to-band observations (this uses the intent DIFFGAINREF, 

26 as implemented since PL2024). The solint is fixed as 'inf' (i.e. 

27 solutions are made per scan). Spectral window combination is governed by 

28 the hm_spwmapmode options described below. In standard pipeline operation 

29 ``auto`` is used - basing spw combination upon SNR and flagging level. 

30 These reference phase solutions are later applied on-the-fly while solving 

31 the (band-to-band) ``phase offset``. The premise being that the ``reference`` 

32 solutions correct for atmospheric phase variability. 

33 

34 - ``phase offset`` (i.e. the band-to-band correction) calibration uses the 

35 "on-source" spectral setup, the "high" frequency for band-to-band observations. 

36 The above ``reference`` phase corrections are applied on-the-fly using a 

37 linearPD interpolation - this corrects (scales) the phases according to the 

38 different ratio of the frequency bands. The solint = 'inf' and each group of 

39 scan blocks (combining groups of DIFFGAINSRC HF scans typically at the start 

40 and end of the observation) are combined respectively. The phase offset 

41 solution generally comprises of 2 time solutions per spw, per polarization 

42 (effectively the instrumental offset between bands). If the observation is long, 

43 approaching two hours, it is possible that there is an additional DIFFGAIN 

44 observation also at the mid-point of the observations, thus making 3 scan groups, 

45 and 3 solutions per spw per polarization. Spectral window combination is governed 

46 by the hm_spwmapmode options described below. ``auto`` is used - basing spw 

47 combination upon SNR and flag data. These solutions are stored in the 

48 pipeline context for later application to the TARGET and CHECK intent(s). 

49 

50 - ``residual offset`` solutions are produced by applying both ``reference`` and 

51 band-to-band ``phase offset`` solutions to the 'on-source' DIFFGAIN intent 

52 on-the-fly, and subsequently solving phases per spw, per scan using 

53 solint='inf'. Spectral window combination is governed by the hm_spwmapmode 

54 options described below. ``auto`` is used - basing spw combination upon SNR 

55 and flagging level. 

56 

57 Residual solutions pre-apply all corrections and solve for the scan-based DIFFGAINSRC phases. These are 

58 designed to scatter about zero degrees with no drift, as shown in the figure below (right panel). Residuals 

59 not limited by SNR should ideally be within +/-30 deg; good-conditions data should be within +/-50 deg. 

60 There should be no significant offset or outlier solutions from the base zero degree phase. 

61

67 

68 Plots in the hifa_diffgaincal weblog. The first set (left) shows the diffgain phase correction to be applied to the 

69 target source (the band-to-band offset). A plot is shown for each spectral window, with phase correction data 

70 points plotted per antenna and correlation as a function of time. The phase offsets are computed by pre-applying 

71 the LF scan based phase-only solution to the diffgain calibrator data and computing new phase solutions for each 

72 spw while combining scan groups at the start and end of the EB. The second set (right) shows the diffgain phase 

73 residuals as a function of time. The residual phase solutions should scatter about zero with no drift and are 

74 calculated by pre-applying the LF to HF phase solutions and the band-to-band offset. The new solutions are not 

75 applied to the target. 

76 

77 As of PL2025, low-SNR heuristics allow ``combine='spw'`` to be used in any of the three solve steps. 

78 The gaincal workflow and low SNR logical flow are shown in the figure below. 

79

83 

84 Left: gain solution workflow with low-SNR heuristic before each ``gaincal``. 

85 Right: low-SNR heuristic logic for spectral window combination. 

86 

87 The heuristic is triggered if: 

88  

89 1. ``combine='spw'`` was triggered as required for the phase-up (in :func:`~pipeline.hifa.cli.hifa_spwphaseup` 

90 for the DIFFGAIN intent - using the relevant REF or the SRC). The previously computed SNR and ``solint`` 

91 are used to calculate whether spw combination is required for the scan based phase solves, or in the case 

92 of the band-to-band offset - the group of scans, as to meet an SNR of 5.0. If this SNR threshold is not met, 

93 spw combination is triggered. 

94 2. A temporary ``gaincal`` shows that (i) not all spw solutions are made; (ii) the fraction of flagged solutions 

95 exceeds 0.5 of the total solutions, and (iii) the fraction of missing scans exceeds 0.7 of the expected scans. 

96 

97 Notes: 

98 QA sub-scores: 

99 

100 - 1.0 if each caltable (reference, band-to-band offset, residual) is successfully produced; 

101 0.0 if any table is missing (which invalidates the calibration). 

102 - 0.9 (informative only) if ``combine='spw'`` was required for any of the three solve steps; 

103 1.0 otherwise. 

104 

105 Only used in band-to-band (diffgain) recipes. 

106 

107 Examples: 

108 1. Derive SpW phase offsets from differential gain calibrator. 

109 

110 >>> hifa_diffgaincal() 

111 

112 """
