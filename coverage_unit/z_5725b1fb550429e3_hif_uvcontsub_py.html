6def hif_uvcontsub(vis=None, field=None, intent=None, spw=None, fitorder=None, parallel=None): 

7 """Subtract the continuum from the uv-data using the ranges in ``cont.dat``. 

8 

9 Fits and subtracts the continuum emission for each science target and spw independently using 

10 the LSRK frequency ranges from the ``cont.dat`` file (produced by :func:`~pipeline.hif.cli.hif_findcont`). The 

11 ``cont.dat`` LSRK ranges are translated to the topocentric (TOPO) frame per MS and reported 

12 in the WebLog. 

13 

14 Starting in PL2024 the fit order is ``fitorder=1`` by default, but ``fitorder=0`` is used when 

15 the ``LowBW`` or ``LowSpread`` condition was flagged for that spw in :func:`~pipeline.hif.cli.hif_findcont` (i.e. 

16 the selected continuum bandwidth is very small or the selected channels are not well spread 

17 across the spw). For any spw listed in ``cont.dat`` with no channel ranges specified, the spw 

18 is treated as ``AllContinuum`` (PL2024+) and no line MS output is produced for that spw. 

19 

20 After this stage the original continuum + line data reside in the ``DATA`` column of 

21 ``*_targets.ms``, while the continuum-subtracted data are written to the ``DATA`` column of 

22 the new ``*_targets_line.ms``. 

23 

24 Notes: 

25 QA = 1.0 if a continuum fit table is successfully created; 0.0 otherwise. 

26 

27 Examples: 

28 1. Subtract continuum for all science targets and spws: 

29 

30 >>> hif_uvcontsub() 

31 

32 2. Subtract continuum for a subset of fields only: 

33 

34 >>> hif_uvcontsub(field='3C279,M82') 

35 

36 3. Subtract continuum for selected spws only: 

37 

38 >>> hif_uvcontsub(spw='11,13') 

39 

40 4. Override the automatic fit order per field and spw: 

41 

42 >>> hif_uvcontsub(fitorder={'3C279': {'15': 1, '17': 2}, 'M82': {'13': 2}}) 

43 

44 """
