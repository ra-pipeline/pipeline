14 """Create cleaned images from the target list populated by :func:`~pipeline.hif.cli.hif_makeimlist`. 

15 

16 In standard ALMA interferometric recipes, this task is invoked multiple times for different imaging 

17 purposes. The scenarios include: 

18 

19 - **Calibrator Images** 

20 The first time it is run in standard recipes is to create per-spw MFS continuum images of the calibrators, 

21 using Briggs weighting with ``robust=0.5``. 

22 

23 - **Polarization Calibrator Images (polarization recipes only)** 

24 Creates and displays per-session aggregate continuum Stokes I, Q, U and V images of all polarization calibrators. 

25 It performs Gaussian fits to each Stokes I, Q, and U image to calculate polarization fraction and angle. 

26 An additional yellow QA score and warning message are given if the Stokes I, Q, or U fits fail. 

27 

28 .. figure:: /figures/polcal_imaging.png 

29 :alt: Polarization calibrator imaging 

30 

31 Example of polarization calibrator imaging, displaying fit results, polarization intensity/angle plots, and Stokes images. 

32 

33 - **Check Source Images** 

34 After creating per-EB, per-spw images of all check sources, the pipeline performs a Gaussian fit and evaluates 

35 positional offsets and flux ratios. Check source imaging uses the dynamic range modifiers for science targets. 

36 An additional QA score is computed as the minimum of two subscores. The 

37 positional-offset subscore is calculated from the separation between the 

38 Gaussian-fitted and cataloged positions in units of the synthesized beam 

39 (``QA_offset = max[0.33, 1 - min(1, offset/beam)]``). The decorrelation 

40 subscore is calculated from the ratio of the fitted peak intensity to the 

41 fitted total flux density 

42 (``QA_decorrelation = max[0.33, 1 - abs(1 - peak/flux)]``). Low values can 

43 indicate potential problems with phase transfer between the phase 

44 calibrator and science targets, but are less diagnostic at low 

45 signal-to-noise ratio or when the check source is farther from the phase 

46 calibrator than the science target. 

47  

48 .. figure:: /figures/checksrc_table.png 

49 :alt: Check source QA table 

50 

51 Example of the check source QA table in the WebLog. 

52 

53 - **Target Per-spw Continuum Images** 

54 Cleaned continuum images are created for each spectral window using the continuum frequency ranges determined 

55 from :func:`~pipeline.hif.cli.hif_findcont`, the ``robust`` value from :func:`~pipeline.hifa.cli.hifa_imageprecheck`, and any size mitigation from :func:`~pipeline.hif.cli.hif_checkproductsize`. 

56 

57 .. figure:: /figures/guide-img030.png 

58 :alt: Target per-spw continuum images 

59 

60 Example of WebLog for target per-spw continuum images. 

61 

62 .. figure:: /figures/guide-img031.png 

63 :alt: View other QA images 

64 

65 Details page displayed after clicking "View other QA images". 

66 

67 - **Target Aggregate Continuum Images** 

68 A cleaned aggregate continuum image (all spws combined) is formed from the ``hif_findcont`` channels. 

69 It is made with ``nterms=2`` if the fractional bandwidth is >= 10%. 

70 

71 - **Target Cubes** 

72 Cleaned continuum-subtracted cubes are created for each science target and spectral window at the native 

73 channel resolution (unless channel binning was selected).  

74 

75 The line-free spectral ranges determined by :func:`~pipeline.hif.cli.hif_findcont` 

76 are assessed by creating moment 8 (maximum value along the frequency axis) and 

77 moment 10 (minimum value along the frequency axis) images of the line-free ranges of the cube, 

78 referred to as the ``mom8_fc`` and ``mom10_fc`` images. In an ideal case, these 

79 images will only contain noise. To test this, three metrics are calculated (see §7.4.3 of ::cite:`2023PASP..135g4501H`):  

80 ``PeakSNR`` = the peak above the median in the ``mom8_fc`` image compared to the median absolute 

81 deviation (MAD) measured in the line-free channels of the cube; ``HistAsym`` = the difference 

82 between the absolute value of the intensity histogram constructed from the ``mom8_fc`` image 

83 with that constructed from the ``mom10_fc`` image; and ``MaxSeg`` = the largest "segment" of 

84 contiguous pixels with values above a threshold in the ``mom8_fc`` image. If ``PeakSNR`` > 5 and 

85 ``HistAsym`` > 0.2, or if ``PeakSNR`` > 3.5 and ``HistAsym`` > 0.05 and ``MaxSeg`` > 1 beam area, 

86 then the QA score is the minimum of 0.65 and the value of an error function between 0.33 and 1.0 

87 based on the fraction of the image represented by the largest segment (resulting in lower QA 

88 scores for larger segments).  

89 

90 These scores are defined to identify if there is noticeable emission in the ``mom8_fc`` image, 

91 but that does not necessarily mean that something has gone wrong with continuum subtraction or 

92 cube imaging. It could be that there is spectrally unresolved line emission, or a line forest, 

93 or that there are low SNR features that are missed by the :func:`~pipeline.hif.cli.hif_findcont` algorithm. 

94 Yellow QA scores identify cases that merit manual examination (by looking at spectra through 

95 the cube at locations of peaks in the ``mom8_fc`` image). *In the majority of cases, the cubes 

96 with poor values of this QA score do not need to be regenerated,* because a small change in the 

97 continuum ranges will have little to no effect on uv-based continuum subtraction. But in some 

98 cases one may want to create a new aggregate continuum image to avoid potential line 

99 contamination (the data reducer will do so if they deem it appropriate), or use different 

100 channel ranges when making moment images. 

101  

102 There is a final possible modification to the QA scores for cubes, based on whether there are 

103 significant deviations in the synthesized beam shape: If the major axis differs by more than a 

104 factor of two from the median of all channels, the QA score is reduced by 0.11. If more than 

105 10 channels are deviant, the QA score is reduced by 0.34. 

106

123 - **Representative Bandwidth Target Cube** 

124 If the PI-requested bandwidth for sensitivity is significantly coarser (> 4x) than the native correlator channel 

125 width, an additional cube is created at the PI-requested bandwidth. 

126 For this cube, the line contamination QA score is restricted to a minimum value of 0.67. 

127 

128 The following common task functionality applies to all imaging stages: 

129 

130 - **Image Coordinates** 

131 In all imaging stages, the image is centered on the ICRS equinox 2000 position requested in the 

132 Observing Tool, or the ICRS ephemeris direction evaluated at the time of first integration for 

133 ephemeris objects. For objects with non-zero proper motion or parallax, the coordinates are ICRS 

134 equinox 2000 but for the epoch of observation. The difference between the Source direction and 

135 Field direction shown in the WebLog reflects this parallax/proper motion term. 

136  

137 For irregular mosaics, the ``psfphasecenter`` is explicitly set to prevent poor PSFs or ``tclean`` failures. 

138 

139 .. figure:: /figures/psfphasecenter.png 

140 :alt: PSF phase center for irregular mosaics 

141 

142 Irregularly shaped mosaics are automatically handled by setting the ``tclean`` ``psfphasecenter`` parameter. 

143 

144 - **Full Polarization IQUV Imaging** 

145 In polarization recipes, full polarization Stokes IQUV images are made. The mask and ``tclean`` threshold 

146 from the corresponding Stokes I imaging stage are carried forward and used (with a fallback to no mask 

147 and recomputed thresholds if not found). This mask is only displayed in the Stokes I WebLog. 

148  

149 - **Automatic Clean Boxes (Automasking)** 

150 Images are deconvolved using ``tclean`` with the ``auto-multithresh`` auto-masking algorithm (Kepley et al. 2020), 

151 which mimics interactive cleaning. The masking threshold is the greater of the values calculated based on the 

152 residual rms noise and sidelobe levels. For continuum imaging, if the algorithm prunes all regions (which can 

153 happen for compact, high-SNR emission), ``tclean`` is run again using a fallback clean mask corresponding to 

154 the area above a fraction of the primary beam response (default 0.3). To save time, this fallback is not done 

155 for cube imaging. 

156  

157 The auto-masking parameters vary based on the array and the 75th percentile baseline length (b75): 

158 

159 .. code-block:: text 

160 

161 Parameter 7m 12m b75<300m 12m b75=300-400m 12m b75>400m 

162 noisethreshold 5.0 4.25 5.0 5.0 

163 sidelobethreshold 1.25 2.0 2.0 2.5 

164 lownoisethreshold 2.0 1.5 1.5 1.5 

165 minbeamfrac 0.1 0.3 0.3 0.3 

166 negativethreshold 0.0/15.0 0.0/7.0 0.0/7.0 0.0/7.0 

167 fastnoise False False False True 

168 

169 *(Note: negativethreshold is 0.0 for continuum and the higher value for line imaging. fastnoise=True uses a simple median absolute deviation; fastnoise=False uses the Chauvenet method).* 

170 

171 - **Cleaning Threshold and Dynamic Range**: 

172 Images are cleaned to `2 x (predicted rms noise) x (dynamic range correction factor)`. 

173 To prevent divergence, if on-source time < 60s and dirty DR > 30 for a cube image, it uses a factor of 5 instead of 2.

241 Notes: 

242 The following base QA behavior applies to ALMA imaging: 

243 

244 - QA = 0.34 when cleaning reports an error or an expected image is not 

245 created. 

246 - QA = 0.0 when the measured image RMS is NaN, indicating that cleaning 

247 diverged. 

248 - For a successfully created image, the image-RMS score compares the 

249 non-primary-beam-corrected noise-annulus RMS with the theoretical 

250 sensitivity after applying the dynamic-range correction. An 

251 error-function scorer gives a score near 1.0 when the ratio is 1 and 

252 approaches its lower limit of 0.34 as the ratio reaches 5. 

253 

254 Additional QA is calculated for specific types of imaging (cube, check source, etc) as detailed above. 

255  

256 Examples:

258

260

262 

263 >>> hif_makeimages(hm_masking='centralregion')
