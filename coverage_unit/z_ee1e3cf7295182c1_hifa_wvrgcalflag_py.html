11 """Generate and apply a WVR phase correction table, flagging antennas with bad radiometers. 

12 

13 Water Vapor Radiometer (WVR) sky brightness temperature measurements in four sub-bands surrounding the 

14 183 GHz water line are converted by the CASA task ``wvrgcal`` into a phase correction table. The phase 

15 rms during observation of the bandpass calibrator, measured with and without the WVR correction, is used 

16 to (1) detect poorly performing WVR units on individual antennas and (2) determine whether the overall 

17 WVR correction is beneficial. 

18 

19 For heterogeneous arrays the task will only attempt a correction if at least ``ants_with_wvr_nr_thresh`` 

20 (default: 3) 12-m antennas have WVRs **and** the fraction of WVR-equipped antennas is at least 

21 ``ants_with_wvr_thresh`` (default: 0.2). If these thresholds are not met, no WVR caltable is created. 

22 

23 For each qualifying MS the workflow is: 

24 

25 1. Generate a gain table from the WVR data. 

26 2. Apply the WVR calibration to the data specified by ``flag_intent``; compute per-scan flagging views 

27 showing the ratio ``phase-rms(with WVR) / phase-rms(without WVR)`` — a ratio < 1 indicates 

28 improvement. 

29 3. Search the views for antennas with anomalously high ratios. If found, recalculate the WVR calibration 

30 with those antennas excluded (``wvrflag``), interpolating results from nearby antennas within 

31 ``maxdistm`` (default: 500 m) provided at least ``minnumants`` (default: 2) are available. 

32 4. If after flagging the remaining WVR-equipped antennas fall below the count/fraction thresholds, 

33 reject the WVR caltable and do not use it in subsequent calibration. 

34 5. If the overall QA score exceeds ``accept_threshold`` the WVR caltable is merged into the context. 

35 

36 For heterogeneous arrays, 7-m (CM) antennas are never removed from the reference antenna list. 

37 

38 The WebLog shows the effects of the phase correction, which antennas (if any) had their WVR data flagged, 

39 per-antenna path-length RMS ('RMS') and channel-to-channel discrepancy ('Disc') values, and a warning if 

40 the correction is not helpful enough to apply. 

41 

42 Notes: 

43 QA is produced per MS via a two-stage metric: 

44 

45 - Stage 1: assess the RMS improvement ratio for BANDPASS and PHASE calibrator sources. The CASA log 

46 lists the ratios of with-WVR phase RMS / without-WVR phase RMS (i.e. 1 / improvement). If the 

47 PHASE calibrator has low SNR with phase RMS > 90 deg on longer baselines, it is excluded and only 

48 the BANDPASS improvement ratio is used. 

49 - Stage 2: cap the score for issues such as flagged antennas, RMS or Disc values > 0.5 mm, low SNR, 

50 or high atmospheric phase variation. The final score is determined by fixed-range linear fits from 

51 the scoring tree shown below. 

52 

53 .. figure:: /figures/hifa_wvrgcalflag_scoring_PL2023.png 

54 :width: 60% 

55 :alt: QA scoring workflow for hifa_wvrgcalflag 

56 

57 QA scoring workflow showing initial and secondary scoring criteria, the resulting 

58 QA metric score range and corresponding color, the meaning of each score, and 

59 instructions for QA analysts reviewing the WebLog. 

60 

61 The final stage score is the lowest score across all MeasurementSets. 

62 

63 Examples: 

64 1. Compute the WVR calibration for all the MeasurementSets: 

65 

66 >>> hifa_wvrgcalflag(hm_tie='automatic') 

67 

68 """
