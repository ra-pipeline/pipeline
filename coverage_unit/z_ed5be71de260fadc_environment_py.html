133class Environment(Protocol): 

134 """ 

135 Environment is a Protocol that collects the attributes that describe a 

136 pipeline execution environment. 

137 

138 This Protocol is intended to help with type safety, ensuring that 

139 implementations for MacOS and Linux provide all required data in the 

140 correct type. 

141 

142 There are several bits of information collected on CPU which are 

143 subtly different: 

144 

145 - logical_cpu_cores and physical_cpu_cores reports how many CPUs are 

146 present on the machine. With SMT, logical CPU cores can be more than 

147 the number of physical cores. 

148 - casa_cores and casa_threads reports how many physical and logical cores 

149 are accessible to the running CASA process, as detected by CASA itself. 

150 

151 On a Linux machine, attributes describing the cgroup limits applied to the 

152 CASA process are also populated: 

153 

154 - cgroup_cpus reports how many CPU cores have been allocated to the 

155 running process, as detected through cgroup limits. 

156 - cgroup_bandwidth reports the CPU bandwidth limits applied by cgroups. 

157 'bandwidth' means the fraction of CPU time for CPUs allocated to the 

158 cgroup, e.g., 25% of cores 1-4 

159 - casa_cores and cgroup_cpus should generally (always?) be equal, but I 

160 don't know enough about the CASA implementation to guarantee that. 

161 

162 The most complex scenario this can describe is, for example, that CASA has 

163 been allocated 25% of cores 1-4 on a machine with 10 physical cores and 

164 14 logical cores. There are other cgroup attributes that could affect the 

165 amount of time CASA receives (e.g., CPU weighting), but those are ignored 

166 for now. We also do not attempt to detect the difference between p-cores 

167 and e-cores, though that may be a factor for non-cluster setups. 

168 """
